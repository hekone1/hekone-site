How It Works

Capture the harvest event. Measure automatically. See the operation live.

HEKONE Origin is designed around the workflow already happening in the field, creating operational intelligence without adding another manual data-collection step.

Process Comparison

Eliminate the weighing step.

Replace a slower 5-step harvest process with a 3-step real-time system that measures while harvesting. Instead of moving material through an added weighing checkpoint, HEKONE Origin captures data where harvest already happens. That means fewer handoffs, faster information, and less delay between collection and decision.

Live Operations

See harvest activity across the orchard as it happens.

Know where bins are, how fast they are filling, and which areas are outperforming or falling behind. Field connectivity is not always perfect, so the system is designed around practical deployment logic: capture locally, sync reliably, and preserve operational visibility when coverage is inconsistent.

This is not just real time when the network is perfect. It is intended to be useful in the field conditions operators actually deal with.

Performance Insight

Know what is making money—and what is losing it.

See output, picking speed, lost value, and action recommendations in real time. HEKONE Origin turns harvest activity into financial insight, so teams can respond before small inefficiencies become larger operational losses.

Operational Alerts

Detect problems before they become losses.

When weight, movement, or handling patterns look abnormal, HEKONE Origin can surface the event immediately and show operators what needs to be checked. This moves the system beyond passive measurement into active operational awareness for shrinkage, handling issues, or irregular movement.

Real-time alerting can help reduce unnoticed loss, improve accountability, and make field events visible before they become larger financial issues.

Trace Back to Source

Trace every problem back to its source.

Connect downstream quality issues directly to the harvest area where they started. HEKONE Origin links each bin to weight, time, and location, turning delayed discovery into a faster inspection and response path.

Patent Pending Architecture

Engineered at the physical layer.

HEKONE Origin is designed as a structural sensing system, where measurement is embedded directly into the physical architecture rather than added as an external layer.

Measurement built into the bin itself.

The architecture integrates load sensing, electronics, display, power, and wireless communication into a field-ready bin system. This enables source-level capture of weight, time, location, and operational events where harvest activity occurs.
